# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participation must be professional, evidence-oriented, and respectful. Welcome different experience levels, challenge claims rather than people, state uncertainty, and give reproducible technical feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data or credentials, publishing another person's personal information, and sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, code, or other contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be made privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile; do not include sensitive evidence in a public thread.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
